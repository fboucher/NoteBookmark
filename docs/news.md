# News & Updates

Welcome to NoteBookmark! Check back here for product updates.

## October 25, 2026 - [Version 1.4.5](https://github.com/fboucher/NoteBookmark/releases/tag/v1.4.5)
- **News & Updates on the Home Page**: You can now read the latest NoteBookmark news right from your Home screen, even when you're offline... which you probably already figured out since you're reading this right now! 👋
- **No More Double-Saving**: Fixed an issue where clicking the browser bookmarklet could get a little too excited and save the same article twice.

## September 30, 2026 - [Version 1.4.4](https://github.com/fboucher/NoteBookmark/releases/tag/v1.4.4)
- **One-Click Browser Bookmarklet**: Save articles from your browser faster than ever! Grab the new "Quick Add" bookmarklet from the Settings page to save any webpage in a compact popup window with a single click.
- **Much Faster Mobile Sync**: Syncing on the mobile app is now significantly faster and smoother, downloading only what has changed since your last sync and working in the background without slowing down the app.

## September 19, 2026 - [Version 1.4.3](https://github.com/fboucher/NoteBookmark/releases/tag/v1.4.3)
- **Offline Storage Manager**: Added a new **Storage** tab in the mobile app so you can easily see how much space your offline articles are using and clean up downloaded content whenever you want.
- **Easier Text Size Controls**: Added `+` and `-` buttons next to the text size slider in the article reader to make adjusting the font size easier.
- **More Reliable Offline Sync**: Improved how articles download so sync doesn't get stuck on unreachable pages, and improved how reading notes are saved offline.

## September 5, 2026 - [Version 1.4.2](https://github.com/fboucher/NoteBookmark/releases/tag/v1.4.2)
- **Reader Controls at Top & Bottom**: Added a Back button and text size controls at both the top and bottom of the article reader, so you don't have to scroll all the way back up when you finish reading.
- **Live Sync Progress**: The Posts page now displays a live progress bar and status message while your articles and offline reading content are syncing.
